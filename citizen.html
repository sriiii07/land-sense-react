What To Do During a Landslide

  • Move to higher ground immediately if you hear rumbling sounds
  • Stay away from slopes, riverbanks, and steep hillsides
  • Do NOT use roads that pass under or near slopes
  • Keep an emergency kit ready: water, food, torch, radio, first aid
  • Charge your phone fully during heavy rains
  • Listen to official announcements — SAFEGROUND alerts are verified by an officer
  • Help elderly and disabled neighbours evacuate first
  • After landslide: check for injuries, avoid damaged buildings, report to authorities
