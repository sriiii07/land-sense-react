What is SAFEGROUND AI?

A prediction-to-response platform built for landslide-prone districts. It doesn't just forecast risk — it closes the loop from satellite data to a citizen standing at a shelter door.

01

Sense

Rainfall, soil moisture, slope, and land cover are pulled continuously from NASA GPM/SMAP, IMD forecasts, and terrain models for every village.

02

Predict

A trained XGBoost model scores landslide probability hourly, mapped to Low / Moderate / High / Critical risk levels per village.

03

Verify

A disaster officer reviews the AI's explanation and live conditions on the authority dashboard, then approves or rejects the alert.

04

Respond

Approved alerts reach citizens instantly with the nearest shelter and route. Rescue teams are prioritized by risk × population exposure.

How it works

From raw sensor data to a citizen at a safe shelter — every step, accounted for.

Data ingestion

Hourly rainfall, soil moisture, and forecast data are ingested and cleaned automatically.

Risk scoring

The model computes a landslide probability per village and explains its top contributing factors.

Officer review

High and Critical risk villages surface on the authority dashboard for a human decision — approve or dismiss.

Citizen alert

Approved alerts push instantly to registered citizens with evacuation instructions and the nearest shelter.

Rescue prioritization

Citizens report Safe or Need Help; rescue teams are dispatched to the highest-exposure villages first.

Built for real disaster response

Not a demo dashboard — a decision-support system a District Disaster Management Authority could deploy.

AI Risk Prediction

XGBoost model trained on rainfall, soil moisture, slope, land cover, and historical landslide records, evaluated to 91% ROC-AUC.

Human-in-the-Loop Approval

No alert reaches a citizen without officer review. Every approval or rejection is logged for accountability.

Live Risk Map & Explanations

Color-coded village risk map with SHAP-based explanations, so officers see exactly why a village was flagged.

Shelter & Capacity Tracking

Real-time shelter occupancy so evacuation routing never sends citizens to a shelter that's already full.

Citizen Status Monitoring

Registered citizens report Safe / Need Help, giving rescue teams a live, village-level picture of who needs help.

Offline-Ready Citizen App

Shelter maps and instructions cache on-device, so evacuation guidance still works when networks fail.
